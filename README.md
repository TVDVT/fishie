# Fishie

Starbie is a tiny motion-controlled digital pet: basically a desktop Tamagotchi with minimal gameplay buttons. Instead of pressing controls, you interact with it by physically moving the board.

*[This guide from Half Life](Week%201%20Guide.md)! was used to complete this project!*

## What it is
Fishie is designed to sit on your desk, show its personality on a small display, and react to how you handle it. Tilting, moving, and otherwise checking in on your pet are meant to be part of the experience.

The screen contains a little koi fish that swims freely from left to right. You can pet it, feed it and play with it using both the key switches and a tilting mechanism. As you play with it, its energy decreases, making him sleepy. Thankfully, there is an option to let the fishie sleep :D. Its love also increases as you play with him and pet him so keep him busy!

The board includes a microcontroller, display, motion sensor, and environmental sensor in a compact PCB design. It is USB powered and built around beginner-friendly through-hole soldering for the add-on components.

## Why?

This project was created for [halflife's](https://halflife.hackclub.com/) first week! Instead of doing a Starbie, I wanted to make it my own and therefore decided to have a little fish in a fish bowl because out of all the pets, fishies are the most low maintenance but very beautiful, especially koi fishies! I do have four fish in a fish tank at home  but having one right on your desk without having the risk of water spilling on all your electronics is a lot better, and it takes less space too!

## Project status

The PCB design, renders, and an Arduino starter firmware are included here. The default program is a koi fish with a tilt-controlled radial menu, hidden stats, and a shake reaction.

## Software

[`Firmware/`](Firmware/) contains one self-contained Arduino IDE sketch for the XIAO ESP32-C3. Builders customize pins, menu actions, starting values, and motion settings together at the top of the sketch. The folder also includes a draggable [browser simulator](Firmware/simulator.html). See the [firmware README](Firmware/README.md) for setup notes.

## Repository contents

- `PCB/` - KiCad source files and fabrication exports
- `Renders/` - front and back PCB renders
- `Firmware/` - Arduino IDE starter sketch
